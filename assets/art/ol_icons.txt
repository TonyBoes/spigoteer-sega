; ol_icons: the soundtrack visualiser's four channel icons (src/jukebox.c; the one-line menus share their other tiles through assets/art/ol_tiles.txt). Four BG tiles, 8x8, drawn on each channel's own palette on the
; wood wall: '.' 0 the channel's bright colour, '#' 3 the wall; '-' 1 and '+' 2 are not used (the bar tiles use them for highlight and shade).
; Hand drawn, every pixel placed here by hand and typed into this grid (tools/gen_ol_tiles.py only transcribes the grid to 2 bpp; no mirroring, no fill, no generator).
; Order: SQUARE 1 (a wide pulse, 50% duty), SQUARE 2 (a pulse train, narrow 25% duty), WAVE (one period of a sine), NOISE (jagged static).

;     comb of two 2 px pulses on a baseline, clearly not the same shape; the two are different enough to tell apart at 1x and on a DMG. No change.
;  2. WAVE: the steps connect diagonally only (no L-shaped doubles), crest on columns 1-2, trough on columns 5-6; at x8 it reads as a sine, at 1x as a "~". The one weak spot
