; Spigoteer: Duke's ENTRANCE (X1 "skid and vault"; owner-approved 2026-10-12, with two changes: his boss face, and a complete empty bike).
; Original art for this project. Built into the game by tools/gen_boss.py (BOSSES duke `entrance`) and src/boss_duke.c.
; Format: named blocks (@NAME), one character per pixel as the other boss art: '.' transparent, '-' cream, '+' ginger, '#' black.
; CGB: OBJ palette 5 = a copy of Duke's BG palette 4 (cream #F8E0C8, ginger #D06030, black #201018); the helmet is OBJ palette 7 (his
; topper's red / white / dark). DMG: OBP1 0xE4 for all, so the bike and Duke carry his BG body's greys.
; The face: the rider's head is the boss's head at the same scale, pixel for pixel (the face rows and the forked beard of WALK in
; duke.txt, cols 11-28 rows 0-14, the jacket and the fist around them left out; a black edge where the jacket was his outline);
; the helmet is the boss's own topper dome (HAT_STILL rows 2-7) in its own 8x16 sprite (@HELM: the dome in rows 8-13 of the 16).
; RIDE and BIKE 48x32 (6 columns x 2 rows of 8x16 sprites), HOP 24x32. The riderless BIKE is a whole motorcycle of its own: frame (a
; backbone under the tank, a down tube, a seat post, a swing arm), a black sprung seat, a chrome teardrop tank with a stripe and a
; filler cap, the V-twin, the neck and fork, the ape hangers and the upswept pipe: cream chrome and black, no ginger (that is Duke's).
; Helmet entry: RIDE at (+14, -8) from the frame's top-left, HOP at (+7, -8). DUST1-3 (the skid and the landing puff), SMOKE1-2 (the rev).
