; Background tiles every screen shares (original art for this project): HUD glyphs, lives mug, banner panel.
; One character per pixel: . = 0 (HUD text), - = 1, + = 2, # = 3.
; '@name' starts a block; a block may be any multiple of 8x8 and is cut into tiles in row-major order.
; BG palette 1 (HUD), see src/main.c. The HUD is light text ('.') on a dark band ('#').
; The stage, bar, door, keg, spout and floor tiles come from the venue art (assets/approved/venues/, M9) and load
; over fixed slots at each level start (tools/gen_art.py).
