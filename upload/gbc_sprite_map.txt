GBC BAR GAME - SPRITE SHEET MAP
===============================

Coordinates are in pixels from the top-left corner of each native sheet (x, y, width, height).
Every sprite uses 3 colors + transparent. Palette order: color 1 (dark/outline), color 2 (mid), color 3 (light).
Colors are snapped to the GBC's 15-bit RGB range. Transparent pixels are alpha 0 in the PNG.
