Owner, 2026-10-21 (patron round 2, Q6): "Always assume they should get another pass for quality improvements unless I indicate they are locked."

**Why:** he treats proposals as drafts. Importing "as is" was never his default.
**How to apply:**
- After he picks art, schedule a quality pass with the skill (briefs.md template, one figure first) before import.
- Only skip it when he says a piece is locked or final.
- Don't ask "pass or import as is?" again.

A pass is NOT a redraw. On 2026-10-21 I briefed a "redraw from scratch" of the round-1 outlaw the owner had praised, because it was made with the forbidden outline script. He preferred the original and asked why I changed it.
- When the owner likes a piece, keep it as is.
- A quality pass keeps its design and look and fixes only flagged problems, by hand.
- If I think a redraw is needed, ask first and keep the original beside it.

Before any art pass, ask how the owner reads the figure, or record it. He reads tiny details differently from the agents. The round-2 outlaw "clean-up" removed what he sees as the ear, and called scarf buttons "eyes". The readings are recorded in scratchpad saloon_patrons/potentials/README.md.
