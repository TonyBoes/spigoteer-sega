On 2026-11-06 the owner said: "I can't read the labels on your images because they are so small."

**Why:** he reviews on a phone, and the sheets' column labels (default small font over wide images) shrink to nothing.

**How to apply:**
- Every sheet brief asks for big column labels: at least about 3% of the image width, bold, high contrast. A short letter per column (A, B, C) is fine.
- Keep sheets to 2-3 columns where possible.
- On the Open Questions card, put the column order in the heading above the image (e.g. "A | B | C") and refer to columns by position as well as name.
