On 2026-10-09 the owner said the other artist "is not good". All sprite and art work is done by our own agents, with proposals in the scratchpad that the owner approves before import. The art_handoff package in the scratchpad is no longer in use.

**Why:** the owner judged the external artist's quality insufficient.
**How to apply:** don't suggest or prepare hand-offs to an outside artist. Plan art rounds as in-house proposals with x8 previews.
