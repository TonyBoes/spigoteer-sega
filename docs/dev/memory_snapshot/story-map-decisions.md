These are the owner's decisions for the story-mode overworld map (2026-10-18). It is a proposal, not built yet.

- **Layout.** The town with the fountain is in the middle. The 10 venues sit around it, each in a fitting biome (forest for the lodge, snow for the igloo, and so on). Thin dashed paths show the route only. The D-pad snaps Sal to the next stop in that direction; he does not walk the paths. It is NOT a stage-select menu.
- **Festival.** The festival games unlock gradually, with an indicator when a new one is available. Each festival gold won in the CURRENT story run is +1 Pour-Off life, capped at +3. Golds from EASY, Arcade, Classic, Practice or earlier runs never count, and the lives are not spent when used.
- **Replay.** A cleared venue replays its mini-game only, never its boss. A better medal upgrades the venue's mark.
- **Medal scoring.** Owner's idea: mini-games score fixed points by medal level, and the medal points are added at the end of the story to make the final score.
- **Warm-up.** The mandatory Perfect Pour warm-up is dropped. Before the Pour-Off, Dregs nudges the player to warm up, a last chance to win more golds.
- **Q61 (2026-10-18).** v4 is the right direction. Stops are coloured dots (green open, red locked) beside larger multi-tile venue vignettes. There is no border or HUD on the map. The gold direction arrows stay. The Chrome Cow is on a road with a cow field behind it. The UFO flies beside a desert mountain with a military air base below.
- **The cap (2026-10-19/20).** Aurora knits Sal a cream WOOLY CAP (a newsboy cap; the owner's portrait). It replaced the pink bobble hat, which had replaced the mustache cozy. He wears it only in story pictures and portraits, never on his in-game sprite.
- **Art.** Build it from Spigoteer's own art style. Do not copy Mario 3's map, which was only a reference for the idea. Earlier drafts were rejected for looking unlike the game's art.

**Why:** the owner iterated through four mock-up rounds to reach this.
**How to apply:** brief map agents from these rulings, and check the v4 map's open questions (Q61) before building. Related: [[art-in-house]], [[three-modes-decisions]].

UPDATE 2026-11-06 (owner): "Fog is not an important feature. If it ends up making a difference, it can be cut." The map fog is optional: if it costs the route plan (ROM, speed or complexity), cut it rather than design around it.

UPDATE 2026-11-06 (owner): "When we reconfigure the map, I want the route design to be a little better. The locations still need to be in the same order as the player loops around, but they don't have to be in the exact positions they are currently in." Venue positions and roads may move in a map reconfiguration; the loop order of the venues is fixed.
Owner 11-06, same topic: "It might be good to avoid the cross roads that exist in this version." The new layout avoids crossroads (no 4-way junctions).
