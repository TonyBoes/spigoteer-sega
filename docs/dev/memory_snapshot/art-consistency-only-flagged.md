On 2026-11-04 the owner asked to fix the clock tower's height relative to the tavern. The artist lowered the tavern roof instead, and the owner said "the roof was not the problem." He also noticed that the coffee booth appeared in only one picture, and that bricks appeared by day but not at sunset.

**Why:** consistency across the town pictures is what he has asked for from the start (Q63). Changes he didn't ask for undo things he already approved.

**How to apply:** in every art brief:
- Name the exact part to change. When the fix could go either way, say which side moves, keeping what he approved and changing the flagged thing.
- Require a consistency audit: buildings, props and texture are identical across all times of day, and only the lighting changes.
