A patron back view must be a real back that plainly matches its front: the same hair mass and silhouette, real back details (a robot gets hatches or vents, a tiger gets clean stripes that follow the form), and nothing at eye height that reads as a face. "Front minus the face" was rejected (PEPPER), and so were noisy stripes (RAJA) and sloppy pixels (PIP). On 2026-10-30 the owner rejected 6 of 8 agent-drawn backs. I had rated 4 of those 6 acceptable or borderline.

**Why:** the owner judges art more strictly than my first read. A lenient verdict from me wastes one of his review rounds.

**How to apply:** in back briefs, name the good pairs to study and ask for 2 versions. Before calling any art acceptable, check it at x8 against the front for match, face-reads, stray pixels and DMG noise. Related: [[art-another-pass-default]], [[face-pass-minimal]].
