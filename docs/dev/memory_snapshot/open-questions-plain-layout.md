Owner 2026-11-08: "Your numbering and lettering on the open questions is confusing. It's really hard for me to tell what you are actually asking and what the illustrations correspond to."

**Why:** cards mixed option letters (A-E, C3), agent codes and stale context; pictures sat in a heap under a list, with their own letter titles.
**How to apply:** one question per card, phrased as what he decides; choices named in plain words (numbered only as 1, 2, 3 within the card), each with 1-3 bullets and its own picture directly under it; my recommendation as a tag on the choice; an "Answer like: ..." line at the end. Crop agent letters/codes off pictures. Reference material in a separate "for reference" section. A question waiting on a picture stays off the page until the picture exists.
