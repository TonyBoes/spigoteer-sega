BOSS INSTRUCTION TEXTS: REVIEW AND PROPOSAL (2026-11-03)
Branch review/boss-texts. PROPOSAL ONLY: no source was changed. Owner ask 2026-11-03: "the text instructions for big tex seem a little incoherent. Can you review all of the boss instructions?"

LIMITS (read from the code)
- Card page (src/mg_card.c, drawn by mg_card_page / line()): a title (the boss name, at most 18 letters) and EXACTLY 4 lines, on rows 7, 9, 11, 13 (a blank row between). A line is at most 18 glyphs (20 minus the frame's two columns); a longer line is drawn over the frame, as the pending-card banners were, so never use it. An empty line "" is allowed (it draws a blank row), but the layout looks best with all four used.
- Pages: a card may have 1-3 pages (page 1 in cards[], the others in more[]). The prompt reads NEXT on all but the last page.
- Glyphs on a card (mg_card.c glyph()): A-Z, 0-9, space, and . , ! ? ' - :  Nothing else (no % / + = ; " ( ) & #). Lower case does not exist. Digits come from the HUD set.
- Banner (story.c banner_build / centre): the word BOSS, then line1 and line2 of boss.c rows[], each at most 20 glyphs (the window row), the same character set (st_glyph: A-Z 0-9 . , ! ? ' - :). Header comment in boss.c says '<= 20'. Nothing else is shown at the fight start; the HOW TO PLAY card opens by itself the first time a boss appears (page 1; NEXT turns the page) and from the pause menu.
- Checked all proposed lines with a script: max card line 18, max banner line 19; every character is in the set above.

WHERE BOSS INSTRUCTION TEXT LIVES
1. src/mg_card.c cards[] (page 1) and more[] (page 2), card id MG_BOSS_CARD + boss id: the pause menu's HOW TO PLAY in a fight and the first-appearance card (boss.c HOOK_CARD / pause_boss_card). THE TEXT THIS REVIEW COVERS.
2. src/boss.c rows[] line1/line2: the banner at the fight's start (and in BOSS RUSH). Six of ten are pinned by tests (test_boss_mascot.py:73, _mothership.py:96, _sasquatch.py:46, _duke.py:78, _sumo.py:113, _prototype.py:87): a changed banner needs its test line changed in the same commit.
3. In-fight notes: boss_yeti.c show_note("TAP FROZEN") (40 frames at the blow) and boss.c show_note("CLEAR THE BAR"), plus the Critic's speech bubbles NO! / BAD (art). Fine as they are.
4. src/rush.c: boss_name[] and venue_name[] only; the BOSS RUSH list has no instruction text.
5. docs/minigames_bosses.md 3.3-3.12: design text, not shown in the game; no 'hint' lines exist as such. Its 'Suggested story text' (section 7) and docs/story.md PRE_*/POST_* scenes are story only, they teach no boss rules (the old suggested PRE_SALOON 'WATCH FOR BIG TEX' is not in the script).
6. tests: tests/test_mg_card.py pins the Log Roll card only; the boss cards are not pinned by text, so the changes need no golden for them. Any card screenshot golden would show a changed card as a diff (the check list for the commit that applies this).

THE WORST PROBLEMS
a. 'EMPTY MUG: THROW / FULL MUG: BUSY' is a leftover of the status tag that the owner had removed on 2026-10-17. What it now means is the capacity indicator (one mug icon per hand, bright = room, dark = he holds an empty), and the words 'EMPTY' and 'FULL' are the reverse of what the player sees (a bright mug is the empty one: room). It sits on 8 of 10 second pages and so crowds out the facts that differ per boss.
b. The Critic's page 2 still says 'SHAKING: SENT BACK'. There is no shaking tag any more.
c. Several lines break inside a phrase ('A MUG AT HIS OLD / BAR BREAKS', 'A MUG WHILE HE / REVS STALLS HIM', 'THEN POPS UP WHERE / FOOTPRINTS BLINK'), and 'NO SUCH THING' (Sasquatch's banner) and 'NO FEAR' (Leo's) tell a first-time player nothing.
d. No card tells the player the basics that cost a life: his empties come back and must be caught at the tap; reaching the tap is a life. Only Tex's page 1 said the second.
e. Big Tex's banner says 'RIDES IN'; he walks in (riding a horse is listed as not built).
f. Sumo's card never says how to win (ring-out at the left end). Sasquatch has no goal line.

PROPOSED STRUCTURE
- Page 1: the goal, the boss's one trick, and the one thing that costs a life. Page 2: the details and what hurts.
- NEW, optional page 3 (a shared entry, same four lines for seven bosses: Tex, Mascot, Sasquatch, Yeti, Duke, Leo, Mothership): the mug icons. Text:
      MUG ICONS BY HIM:
      BRIGHT: THROW MORE
      DARK: HE HOLDS ONE
      FULL UP: IT BREAKS
  It replaces the 'EMPTY MUG: THROW / FULL MUG: BUSY' lines. Not for the Critic (his page 2 says it in words), the Prototype (no limit: only the dark icons show) or the Sumo (no limit). Cost: 7 more[] entries, 4 pointers each (about 7 x 9 B = 63 B in the card's bank 255; the strings can be shared) and the PRESS A prompt moves to page 3: the first-appearance card then needs one more NEXT press. If the owner wants only two pages, drop page 3 and the icon legend goes unexplained (the icons are self-evident once you see 3 of them fill up); say so and I will give Tex a legend page 2 instead.
- The banner is the goal in two lines: line 1 the boss or his trick, line 2 the number to beat.
