# Saloon patrons round 2: concepts and pick list

All figures: 16x16 front (3/4 facing right) and back (symmetric about col 8), house format, one palette (no enhanced variant), hand-drawn, cleanup logs in each folder.
Sheets: all8_compare_x4.png / _x1.png (beside the committed trio A, B, C; rows front, back, front + mug; DMG above, CGB below). Mock-ups: mock1..mock4 (_dmg/_cgb, x4 and x1).

## Pick list

1. PROSPECTOR 1 (approved by the owner). Silver beard, ruddy face, black slouch hat. Slot A (title slot: one palette).
2. PROSPECTOR 2. Black beard, pale face and shirt, khaki hat and brim; works as A or C. Differs from 1 by value (dark beard on a light face), hat colour (light) and the shirt. Assumption: the owner has not yet answered the dark-beard question.
3. OUTLAW. Redraw of round 1 d2_dusty C: flat dark hat with rust band, eyes in the brim shadow, rust bandana with a knot on the near side, striped poncho. Slot C.
4. SINGER (B). Tall leaning feather plume, chignon bun at the back-left, bare shoulders, choker, red lips. Silhouette: tall and narrow on top, wide at the shoulders; no hanging hair.
5. RANCHER (B). Very wide flat-brim straw hat, one thick plait over the near shoulder, plaid shirt. Silhouette: a flat horizontal bar on top and a vertical plait.
6. GUNSLINGER (B). Low black hat, popped duster collar wings, steel-blue coat, white shirt V. Silhouette: angular wings, no hair mass.
7. CARDSHARP (B). Sleek black bob (a hard box), green eyeshade visor pointing right, green vest, bow tie. Silhouette: boxy hair + flat visor.

The bonnet idea (round halo, ribbon strings) was drawn and dropped (out/bonnet, not delivered): it read as a hood at 1x.

- A 2, B rancher or gunslinger, C outlaw
