8 of 10venues with a sprite palette free now (OBJ 4)
0background palettes free in any venue

The catch: a patron walking along the bar is drawn in the background, and only becomes a sprite when it is lifted over another patron. So every patron look needs one sprite palette and one background palette with the same three colours. Freeing sprite palettes alone does not add a patron; the background side has to free one too, or the new look has to share a background palette that already has its colours.
